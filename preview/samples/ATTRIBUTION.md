# Bundled sample photographs

These are demonstration photographs, **not the user's personal memories**. Local JPEG derivatives were resized to a maximum dimension of 384 pixels for the browser preview. They are served from this repository, not fetched from a third-party image host at runtime.

Source: scikit-image v0.19.3 sample data, with rights information in its [data fetcher documentation](https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/blob/v0.19.3/skimage/data/_fetchers.py).
